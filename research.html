Research.

Research interests

Areas of interest

I study learning-based physical-layer receivers for OFDM and MIMO systems, especially attention-aided MMSE channel estimation and generative priors for joint channel estimation and decoding. I am interested in closing the gap between strong statistical performance and practical inference complexity.

Selected research directions

Current and recent projects

Model-based learning for channel estimation

Attention-aided MMSE estimators for OFDM and MIMO receivers, learning linear estimation filters while keeping inference computationally efficient.

Learning from imperfect channel labels

Ridge-regularized training and covariance-shrinkage approaches for training estimators when clean channel-state information is unavailable.

Generative signal priors and joint decoding

Diffusion-based channel priors, BCJR decoding, and extrinsic message passing for pilot-limited MIMO systems.
