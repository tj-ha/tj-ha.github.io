Research interests

Areas of interest

My research interests are in wireless, communication, and information theory for AI: wireless system design for distributed and edge learning and inference, AI-native 6G networks, and information-theoretic tools for estimation and learning under communication constraints.

My current work develops the learning side of this agenda at the wireless physical layer, especially attention-aided MMSE channel estimation and generative priors for joint channel estimation and decoding, closing the gap between strong statistical performance and practical inference complexity.

Selected research directions

Current and recent projects

Model-based learning for channel estimation

Attention-aided MMSE estimators for OFDM and MIMO receivers, learning linear estimation filters while keeping inference computationally efficient.

Learning from imperfect channel labels

Ridge-regularized training and covariance-shrinkage approaches for training estimators when clean channel-state information is unavailable.

Generative signal priors and joint decoding

Diffusion-based channel priors, BCJR decoding, and extrinsic message passing for pilot-limited MIMO systems.
