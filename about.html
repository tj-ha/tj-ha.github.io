Background

I am an M.S. student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jeonghun Park. I am also a Visiting Scholar in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, hosted by Prof. Hyeji Kim.

My research sits at the intersection of wireless communications and machine learning, with an emphasis on efficient, model-based learning methods that work under practical channel uncertainty, limited pilot observations, and imperfect training labels.
